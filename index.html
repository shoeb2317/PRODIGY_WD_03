Rules & Game Engine

Objective

The goal is to align three of your markers (either horizontally, vertically, or diagonally) on the 3x3 grid before your opponent does.

Game Modes

vs AI Opponent: Test your wits against Easy, Medium, or the mathematically Unbeatable Minimax algorithm.
Player vs Player: Pass and play locally with customized player profiles and live turn indicators.

Minimax Algorithm

The AI simulates all future branches of the game tree. It assigns +10 for an AI win, -10 for a player win, and 0 for a tie, subtracting depth to favor quicker victories. It will never make a losing blunder!
